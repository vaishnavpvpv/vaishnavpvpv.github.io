MATERIAL 01 · WELL-DRIED HARDWOOD

Nilambur teak (തേക്ക്)

01 / 05

A well-known wood from Kerala.

Warm wood grain for furniture, doors, and small details around the home.

ABOUT THIS MATERIALNatural grain · well-dried hardwood
GOOD FORMain doors, dining sets, prayer units, and grooved panels
TEAK DETAILSCan last 60+ years · handles damp air well · has natural oilsMain doors, dining sets, prayer units, and grooved panels · Matte PU (a clear wood coat), teak oil rubbed by hand, or walnut finish
Choosing wood for the rainy seasonWell-dried solid wood has natural oils and a tight grain (the lines in the wood). BWP (boiling-water resistant) marine plywood is made to resist water. Both need the right material, protected edges, strong joints, and a good finish for Kerala's rainy season. Compare these with ready-made furniture that you put together or MDF (a board made from wood fibres) before you decide.

VIMAL KUMAR · FOUNDER

VIMAL KUMAR · HIS STORY IN FOUR PARTS

A life spent
working with wood.

From learning carpentry in Kerala to starting VAISHNAV Furniture Works.

01

His first tools: a chisel & muzhakkole (മുഴക്കോൽ)

He learned to take his time, follow the wood grain, measure with a muzhakkole (a traditional measuring stick), and make each first cut with care.

02

Across Kerala: making carved teak doors

He learned the shapes, sizes, and wood joints used in Kerala's carved teak doors and other traditional woodwork.

03

Years in Arabia

Working abroad taught him new ways to plan projects and finish wood for different homes.

04

Starting VAISHNAV Furniture Works

Chosen with his father's love and faith, the name became a workshop for made-to-fit furniture and home woodwork, shaped by a lifetime of working with wood.
