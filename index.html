FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions Homeowners Often Ask Us

Do you visit our house site for measurements?

Yes, absolutely. We come directly to your new house site in Karukutty, Angamaly, Azhakam, Chalakudy, and surrounding areas to take exact wall-to-wall measurements before preparing quotes or starting work.

Can we choose our own wood and plywood?

Yes. You can choose whether you prefer 100% solid timber (Teak, Anjili, Mahogany) or BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Marine Plywood with premium laminates or acrylic finishes for your kitchen and wardrobes.

Can we show you designs from Pinterest or architectural drawings?

Yes! Many clients bring photos from Pinterest, Instagram, or their architect's 3D drawings. With 26 years of carpentry mastery, we can bring those exact designs to reality for your specific space.

How long does a full home interior woodwork project take?

Depending on the size (e.g. 3BHK or 4BHK), standard kitchen and bedroom wardrobes usually take 3 to 5 weeks from measurement to final installation.
